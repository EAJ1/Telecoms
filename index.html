Ask us something
What’s the difference between internet and Wi-Fi?

Your internet service brings a connection to your property. Wi-Fi shares that connection wirelessly between your router and devices. Your experience depends on both.

Can I check coverage here?

You can send a coverage enquiry with your suburb or town. The team needs to review your location before confirming availability, connection type or installation.

Which connection is right for me?

Tell us how many people use your connection and whether you work, stream or game at home. For businesses, include your team size and any critical applications in your enquiry.

Can I download the My Imbali app?

Not yet. The app on this page is an interactive concept using sample data. You can register your interest through the contact form, but there is no announced launch date.

Are prices and installation included?

Pricing, equipment, installation charges and contract terms are supplied in your quote after your requirements and location have been reviewed.
